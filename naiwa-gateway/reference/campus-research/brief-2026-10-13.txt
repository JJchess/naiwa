NAIWA CAMPUS — RESEARCH, NOT IMPLEMENTATION
Date: 2026-10-13

User brief: Evolve the redirect experience into a high-quality playful promotional
website. Each department should have distinct character and its own Naiwa mascot.
Research broadly before implementing. No changes to current production or V10.

Active prior judgments:
- Clean presentation, few small labels, tasteful nonofficial identity.
- Strong emblem construction and cohesive brush title; mascot remains focal.
- Motion follows mascot rhythm; avoid ponderous pacing and forced scrolling.
- No elastic overscroll on root or nested containers; preserve ordinary input.
- Public mobile accessibility is a first-class requirement, given DNS history.

Open questions, asked asynchronously:
- Tone: absurd humor, cinematic spectacle, or combination.
- Departments: Tsinghua-inspired, fictional, or a mixture.
Provisional direction: polished visual craft with playful content; sample discipline
categories are creative proposals, not claims about official departments.

Recommendation:
Build a campus open-day hub plus department theatres. Begin with one polished
mechanical-engineering theatre and one contrasting art/architecture theatre.
Each department needs unique silhouette/prop, setting, action, playful consequence,
short meaningful subject content, and a shareable takeaway.
Consistent mascot base, lighting, material, typography and navigation across scenes.
Show content even when expensive scene rendering is loading or unavailable.
Main site should stop automatic 6-second external navigation; keep original
redirect at a separate route if desired. This is a proposal only, not implemented.

Validation to require before launch:
- Real mobile Safari/Android/WeChat and non-VPN networks; keep DNS diagnostics
  separate from renderer/performance failures.
- Touch equivalents for pointer interactions, optional sound, reduced motion.
- Native scroll boundary tests; WebGL failure still exposes department text/links.
- Performance budgets established on prototype, measured before promising speed.
- Ability to directly open/share each department, restore Back navigation.

Evidence limits:
Research uses live-page text, published creator explanations and indexed showcases.
No full interactive browser session, frame-rate benchmark or mobile comparison was
available in this environment. No proprietary code/assets copied, no licenses
assumed for future reuse, and no implementation or deployment changes made.
